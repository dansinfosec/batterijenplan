# CONTENT GAP REPORT — V6 (2027 article)

Based on Ubersuggest SERP analysis (thuisbatterij na 2027 · terugleverkosten) and content_ideas, 2026-08-03.

## Who ranks now

- **Official / consumer orgs:** rijksoverheid.nl (DA81, law), consumentenbond.nl (DA66), eigenhuis.nl (DA53),
  independer (DA56). Strong authority, but they explain the *rules*, not the *euro value of a battery*.
- **Vendors / manufacturers:** zonneplan.nl (DA38), solarwatt.nl, ankersolix.com, blenditpower.com. Product-led,
  qualitative "loont het nog?" framing.
- **News / community:** solarmagazine.nl, youtube, reddit, forums. Timely but shallow on quantification.
- **Suppliers (terugleverkosten SERP):** Essent, Vandebron, Greenchoice, Energiedirect — own-tariff pages.

## The gaps (what nobody does well)

1. **Total value vs incremental 2027 value confused.** Competitors quote a battery's whole value or a
   payback, but do not isolate what the *2027 policy change* adds. → We show A/C/D and the D−A step.
2. **"A battery removes terugleverkosten" stated flatly.** Few explain that this is only true for a
   **per-kWh** fee; a **fixed** fee is unavoidable and **staffels** only help at a boundary. → Fee-structure section.
3. **Policy effect and fee avoidance are added together as one number.** → We separate them (€405 vs €202)
   and show the interaction is €0 (and why, and its scope limit).
4. **Max self-consumption + max trading revenue summed.** Marketing pages imply you get both at once. → We
   show reserving capacity for trading *costs* self-consumption (reserve opportunity-cost distribution).
5. **Unverified onbalans marketing figures presented as income.** → We treat imbalance as an explicit
   SCENARIO OVERLAY on an external, unverified reference, with a break-even *range* (not a single "25%").
6. **Day-ahead and imbalance conflated.** → We state they are different markets and keep them separate.
7. **Battery-size diminishing returns omitted.** → We show the median policy effect barely rises above ~10 kWh.
8. **No transparent simulation / methodology.** No competitor publishes a reproducible model. → Our core
   differentiator: 35.040-interval simulation, 25 households × 4 batteries × 8 fee structures, tests + red team.
